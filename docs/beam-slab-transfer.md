# Slab loads carried by beam formwork

Select **D3 Shoring System**, include the applicable left/right slab, then select **Transfer slab loads through beam supports**. Enter each clear slab bay and the upper support spacing along the beam. This mode assumes rectangular bays between matching parallel beam supports with mirrored upper-foot positions. The beam concrete is present before the slab loads arrive. Upper supports are ideal vertical load-transfer feet; they are not modeled as D3 towers.

The existing adjacent slab-strip calculation remains available with transfer mode off. Saved calculations without transfer fields retain that behavior.

## Load path

1. Double-H20 upper bearers take the area load over their actual stock length and each row's tributary width. One uncut available stock length is used across each bay, leaving 200–350 mm at each concrete beam face. Longer bays requiring upper joints are not supported by this first model. Their stock, coverage and mirrored foot positions are searched together. Foot centres must be at least 250 mm from a beam face and 150 mm from the upper stock ends. The search maintains at least 300 mm between the two upper feet; this is a search bound, not a D3 frame dimension.
2. A double-H20 lower transverse bearer sits at every upper-support row. It receives the beam load over that row's closing tributary strip, plus concentrated loads at the actual upper-foot centres. Lower stock placement is searched, including asymmetric placement for a slab on one side. Both primary lines and all feet have at least 150 mm of stock beyond their centres. These centre allowances do not verify the bearing contact detail.
3. The lower bearer's two reactions become point loads on the main longitudinal double-H20 pairs. Every independent longitudinal stock is analyzed using those point loads, with at least two supporting leg planes, available uncut stocks, no overlaps and no continuity at member breaks. Transverse rows are kept at least 40 mm from those breaks as a layout search allowance; actual row widths and end details require verification.
4. The actual longitudinal reactions are checked against the entered permissible leg load. Beam-only, beam + left slab, beam + right slab and combined cases are checked as applicable. Negative reactions at an upper foot, transverse bearing or tower leg invalidate a candidate. Reported leg loads are the envelopes across cases, so their sum is not a simultaneous applied load.

All H20s in this transfer model are pairs sharing load equally: moment 10 kNm, shear 22 kN, EI 899.7512 kNm². Relative H20 deflection uses the selected L/500, L/350 or L/270 criterion for each support span and overhang. Legacy fixed-mm limits remain usable in the existing calculator modes, but require selecting a ratio to use transfer mode.

Only actual H20 coverage contributes slab load. For a mirrored two-foot upper bearer, one receiving beam takes half its loaded stock area; the other half goes to the opposite beam. Slab above the concrete beam width is not counted again. Along the beam, the primary stock end allowance defines the loaded length, and the first/last row tributaries close to those stock ends. No excluded edge load is redistributed. Sheathing and slab secondary H20s are not analyzed by this mode; the area load is delivered to upper bearers as a tributary UDL.

## Optimization and drawings

The deterministic bounded search ranks **receiving-beam tower legs first, then total H20 length**, including longitudinal primaries, lower transverse pairs and the shared upper bearer pairs. Upper support rows follow the entered spacing with centred closing strips and small phase adjustments. Manufactured lower D3 frame/cross dimensions, rotated independent towers and unbraced gaps are respected. The standalone-600 exclusion applies to lower towers in either orientation. The search is not an exhaustive proof of the global minimum. Opposing-beam tower legs are not included in the receiving-beam quantities. Dimensions are limited to 30,000 mm in transfer mode.

The plan scrolls horizontally and shows the beam length, each T bearer row, row spacing, upper-foot locations, longitudinal stock breaks, frames/crosses, tower gaps and stock-end-to-leg offsets. The accompanying transverse section shows the concrete faces, upper stock length, clear bay, face-to-foot clearance, upper-stock-end-to-foot inset and lower-stock-end-to-primary offsets. The opposite beam is mirrored. The section is schematic vertically; no upper tower geometry or bracing is implied.

Press **Show shear, moment & deflection**, then select a primary or transverse pair from the main plan. Select an upper pair from the accompanying main section; it uses the identified maximum-tributary row. The diagram window offers load-case buttons for lower members, shows point-load values/locations and plots the exact analysis used to accept the layout. Shear jumps include both sides of support and point-load stations. Each diagram describes a pair, rather than a single H20.

Estimated slab soffit movement includes upper flexure, lower transverse flexure and the interpolated/extrapolated movement of the two lower longitudinal lines at the foot. Two-support transverse and upper bearers are statically determinate, so their support movements add an affine displacement without changing their reactions. The mirrored opposite foot is assumed to have the same movement. Upper displacement extrema are computed from the beam polynomial, not only sampled chart curves. This combined movement is reported separately from relative member criteria; no project limit for total slab movement is assumed.

## Verification boundary

This is a vertical force-transfer and H20 member calculation. Upper-support capacity/stability, baseplates, local bearing and splitting, load sharing hardware, sheathing, slab secondary timbers, tower height/bracing/stability and foundations are outside it. **Passing the numeric checks does not approve the concentrated-load bearing arrangement.** [Doka timber-beam instructions](https://direct.doka.com/_ext/downloads/downloadcenter/999791002_2024_12_online.pdf) restrict heavy concentrated loads combined with wide spans. The pair model uses the [H20 top technical properties](https://direct.doka.com/_ext/downloads/downloadcenter/999642202_2023_01_online.pdf); equal sharing and the actual foot/head detail need to be established for use on site.

The Beam tests check force and moment equilibrium through each transfer interface, support count, pair criteria, stock coverage, one-sided/combined stages, foot clearance, manufactured geometry, exclusion behavior and exact displacement/shear-jump examples. The WPF checks exercise mode input validation, optimization, plan selection, actual point-load diagrams, file round trips, reports and legacy input behavior.
