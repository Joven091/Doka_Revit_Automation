# Crossing secondary H20s supported on existing main primaries

Automatic and Existing supports crossing searches now consider bearing the crossing secondary H20s on the adjacent inner main-primary axes when a stacked secondary arrangement is unavailable. Equal soffits permit direct bearing on one primary; a deeper adjacent beam permits a raised bearing (site box-out) on its primary. Explicit Dedicated towers selection retains its existing meaning.

The solver checks actual primary axes, nonnegative vertical transfer gaps, permitted secondary stocks, stock end setbacks, plywood row spacing, all secondary strength/deflection checks, and full timber/concrete/leg clearances. Crossing rows remain symmetric where possible. Local main-secondary setting-out can move or add rows under the existing bearer assumption, keeping every physical spacing within the checked design pitch. Primary joints must provide the complete 80 mm H20 bearing footprint.

The crossing secondary's bearing reaction goes directly to the selected primary line. It is excluded from the main-secondary analysis, preventing double transfer. Small concrete strips beyond the crossing H20 stock retain the existing checked side-shutter/end-strip path through the main secondaries. Slab edge ledger forces and all concrete-strip forces are conserved and counted once. Final main-primary bending, shear, deflection and individual leg checks use the resolved loads.

Main-primary diagrams retain Beam + Slab UDL outside crossings and Beam + Cross Beam UDL over each full crossing width. Clicked UDL cards show direct crossing-bearing reactions divided by that width alongside any beam-secondary transfer. The box-out has no plan or section solid; the section shows a vertical transfer arrow at the actual primary bearing axis. Box-out member, bearing, connection and stability design is outside scope; its support axis is an engineer-specified assumption, not an approved box-out design.

`--primary-bearings` checks a 1.80 m catalogue H20 across the reported 1.875 m bay, direct bearing on the shallow beam's inner primary, 600 mm raised transfer to the deep beam's inner primary, reaction equilibrium, secondary/primary/leg acceptance and no duplicate secondary loading. `--primary-bearing-benchmark` reproduces the uploaded 2026-10-22 eight-beam inputs with custom lengths disabled and plywood L/350. The ordinary complete search runs without an elapsed-time cutoff; first-complete-candidate and final-return times are reported separately. `--two` selects the isolated two-beam junction, `--first` requests the existing feasibility traversal for diagnosis, and `--prefix` is a cancellable 90-second profiling run rather than a completed benchmark.
